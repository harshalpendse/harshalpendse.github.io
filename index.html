JobTrail

Chrome extension · Manifest V3, no frameworks · Everything stays local, no server

A Chrome extension that keeps a log of the jobs you apply to on LinkedIn, Indeed and Naukri, and tells you when it's time to follow up. It catches the Apply click, saves the role, company and description, and sorts everything into a board of Applied, Follow up, Interviewing, Offers, Ghosted and Rejected. A job that's been sitting for 7 days nudges itself into Follow up and sends a notification. Everything is stored in chrome.storage.local — no account, no server, no analytics.
